The Six Seasons

Exploring the Noongar seasonal calendar

The Noongar six-season calendar describes the natural cycle of Country in the South West of Western Australia through weather patterns and environmental signs. This app compares what the traditional calendar says about each season with real weather data recorded for Perth between August 2025 and July 2026 — showing where the two agree, and where the picture is more complex.
